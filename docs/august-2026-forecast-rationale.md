# August 2026 Forecast Rationale

Decision date: July 1, 2026

## Decision

August 2026 telehealth capacity is targeted at 2,250 provider hours, equivalent to 4,500 appointment slots.

The state-level target table is a flat per-state allocation based on trailing appointment demand from April, May, and projected June. The final row values include a 1-hour token allocation for WY, OK, and NE so every state with expected provider coverage remains represented. To keep the network target at exactly 2,250 hours / 4,500 slots, PA carries the 1-hour rounding adjustment.

## Rationale

The demand floor was approximately 2,800 slots. That was treated as too low for August because recent booked appointment volume had been consistently above that level, and cutting to the floor could jeopardize same-day / next-day access and provider consistency.

The planning ceiling was 4,500 slots, roughly returning capacity to March 2026 levels. March was used as a practical reference point because SLA performance was strong and capacity was meaningfully lower than the Q2 overshoot.

The team chose the ceiling/planning target of 4,500 slots to balance:

- Reducing provider capacity and cost after Q2 overstaffing.
- Preserving same-day / next-day access.
- Maintaining enough provider consistency for August.
- No known large August client launches from growth / fan club context.

## August Scheduling Rules

- Clinical lead / admin providers are accepted first.
- All other telehealth providers are ranked by current hourly rate, regardless of internal vs DirectShifts source.
- DirectShifts percentage-share logic is removed for August.
- DirectShifts NPs in the August scope use a 60-hour floor and 80-hour target when they submit on time through Jotform.
- Hours above state targets or DirectShifts NP targets are held in the overflow list for backup coverage.
- The Jotform priority deadline is July 7, 2026.
